MeMeMe — Privacy policy
Updated: 26 September 2026

Publisher and privacy contact
MeMeMe is published by Achraf MEZDARI, an individual based in France.
Email: achraf.mezdari.games@gmail.com

The current game
MeMeMe is a game for a general audience, not directed at children. Gameplay works offline. This version includes Google AdMob advertising but has no accounts, Google login, in-app payments or cloud saves. It does not request your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. Unity analytics and automatic Unity diagnostics are disabled.

Advertising and privacy choices
When online, Google AdMob may process IP addresses (including approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and SDK diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google receives this information; we do not receive your individual gameplay trajectories. Test advertisements also communicate with Google.

Google's consent form offers choices where required. Ad requests start only when Google's consent platform permits them. Revisit Settings > Ad Privacy when available to change your choices, including withdrawing consent. Refusing consent does not block gameplay. Local Reset Save does not reset Google's privacy choices. Android also provides advertising-ID controls in device settings.

Where required, consent is the basis for advertising storage/access and personalization; other processing depends on the choices and legal bases explained in Google's privacy message. Google's retention, international-transfer safeguards and privacy controls are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks. The publisher does not independently sell player data.

Data on your device
The game stores progress, best times, stars, unlocked levels and skins, earned-credit spending, claimed achievements, and sound, music, vibration and camera-shake and language preferences. These are used only to remember your progress and choices. The save and its recovery copy stay in the app's local storage, protected by the device's normal application-storage controls.

Echo and replay trajectories contain only in-game positions, rotations and timing. They remain in memory for the current run and replay, then are discarded when the run is replaced or the app closes. They are not video or microphone recordings.

Retention and deletion
Local progress remains until you reset it or delete the app's stored data. Settings > Reset Save > Reset Everything clears progression and settings from the active save and its local recovery copy when saving succeeds. If an error appears, free storage and try again. Your operating system or backup service may keep separate copies according to your device settings; manage those with that provider. Restoring a device backup can restore older progress.

If you email us
We receive your email address, message and attachments to answer your request and resolve problems. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information. Ordinary support is based on our legitimate interest in helping players; legally required requests are processed to meet legal obligations. We retain correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations, then delete it.

Support email is handled by the publisher and Gmail/Google. Google may process information outside the EEA under the safeguards described at https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks. Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. This email processing occurs only if you contact us; gameplay does not send email or data to Gmail.

Your rights
Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information we hold, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact the email above. We normally respond within one month; any legally permitted extension will be explained. We cannot remotely access a local save we have never received. You may complain to the CNIL at https://www.cnil.fr or another competent data-protection authority.

Stores and changes
Google Play and your device or backup provider process information for their own services under their own privacy notices. This policy covers the game with AdMob. Before adding Google login or other online features, we will update this policy and provide any required choices. Future features are not active in this version.
